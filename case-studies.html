NovaTech: The Launch That Changed Everything

NovaTech Solutions · 2024

The Challenge

NovaTech Solutions was entering one of the most competitive markets in the world — AI-powered productivity software. With dozens of well-funded incumbents and a launch window of just 6 weeks, they needed content that didn't just introduce a product. They needed content that started a movement.

They came to us with a clear brief: make people feel something about software. That's not easy. Most enterprise tech marketing is cold, feature-driven, and forgettable. We were determined to make NovaTech the exception.

"We didn't want another product demo. We wanted to make people feel what it's like to work without friction — to show the human side of productivity."
— Sarah Chen, CMO, NovaTech Solutions

Our Approach

We spent three weeks in discovery — interviewing NovaTech's team, shadowing power users, and mapping the emotional journey of their ideal customer. The insight that unlocked everything: people don't want to be more productive. They want to be present for the moments that matter. NovaTech gives them back their time.

That became the creative core: Time Back. A cinematic brand manifesto film that told the story of a world slowed down, then powered forward by NovaTech's platform.

What We Delivered

  • 3-minute cinematic brand manifesto film (hero asset)
  • 6-part social documentary series (behind the product)
  • 30-second and 15-second paid media cuts for Meta and YouTube
  • Platform-specific social content calendar (12-week rollout)
  • Full launch distribution strategy and media buy guidance
  • Photography suite for editorial and OOH placements

The Results

Launch week exceeded every benchmark. The hero film generated 12 million organic views across LinkedIn, YouTube, and X without a single dollar of paid promotion behind it. NovaTech's website traffic spiked 340% and held at 180% above baseline through the following quarter.

The 15-second paid cuts ran for 9 months at a 4.2x ROAS, becoming the control creative against which all subsequent NovaTech ads were tested. The campaign was shortlisted for three B2B Marketing Awards and won the Tech Brand of the Year category at the Digital Content Awards.

Apex Fitness: 8x ROAS. $2.4M Revenue.

Apex Fitness · 2024

The Challenge

Apex Fitness had a great product — an online coaching platform — but their ad creative was bleeding budget with sub-2x returns. They'd tried three agencies before us. The content wasn't converting and they were running out of patience.

They gave us 90 days to prove a different approach. We didn't just replace their creative — we rebuilt the entire performance ecosystem from the ground up.

"Every agency promised results. Kinesis actually delivered. The difference was that they understood our customer as well as we did — maybe better."
— Marcus Williams, Founder, Apex Fitness

Our Approach

We ran deep customer research: interviews, survey analysis, and competitive creative audits. The insight: Apex's customers weren't motivated by aesthetics — they were motivated by the fear of falling behind. We built creative around ambition, not vanity.

Then we built a high-volume creative testing system: 24 unique ad concepts across 3 hooks, 4 body variations, and 2 CTAs — deployed across Meta and YouTube in structured A/B tests with weekly iteration cycles.

What We Delivered

  • 24 unique ad creative variants across multiple formats
  • Structured A/B testing framework with weekly reporting
  • UGC-style, testimonial, and cinematic creative formats
  • Platform-specific optimization for Meta and YouTube
  • Monthly creative refresh cadence to combat ad fatigue

The Results

By week 6, we'd identified the control creative. By week 10, ROAS had climbed to 8x. Over the 14-month campaign run, Apex attributed $2.4M in direct revenue to our content. The campaign became their biggest-ever revenue driver, outperforming their SEO and email channels combined.
